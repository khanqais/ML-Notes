**Information Systems (IS)** is a broader framework that combines IT with people, data, and business processes to collect, process, and distribute information for decision-making

## 3. Operational and Strategic Impacts

- **Internet and Network Economics:** Digital networks reduce the cost of storing, copying, and distributing information to near zero. This enables disintermediation, expands market reach globally, and lets firms operate 24/7 digital storefronts.
    
- **Data-Driven Decision-Making:** Business intelligence (BI) and decision support systems (DSS) replace guesswork with empirical analysis. Operational staff get automated alerts, middle managers run scenario models, and executives track enterprise-wide KPIs in real time.
    
- **Competitive Positioning:** Information systems enable firms to lower operational costs (cost leadership), personalize products at scale (differentiation), target micro-segments (niche focus), and raise switching costs by integrating deeply with suppliers and customers.
