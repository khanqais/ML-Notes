Decision Trees are easy to build, easy to use and easy to interpret......but in practice they are not that awesome.
Trees have one aspect that prevents them frombeing the ideal tool for predictive learning, namely inaccuracy. In other words, they work great with the data used to create them, but they are not flexible when it comes to classifying new samples.
