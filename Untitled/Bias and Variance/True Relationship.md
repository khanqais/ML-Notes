The **true relationship** is the underlying pattern between an input X and an output Y in the real world, not just the pattern your model happens to find in its training data. We usually don’t know its exact shape; we use data to estimate it

## Mouse example
Let X be mouse weight and Y be mouse size. The true relationship describes what size tends to be for mice of each weight. It might be a straight line, a curve, or something more complicated. The line fitted by linear regression is our _estimate_ of that relationship, not necessarily the relationship itself.
