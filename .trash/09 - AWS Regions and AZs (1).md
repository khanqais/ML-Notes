# AWS Regions and AZs

- **Region**: geographic area (e.g. ap-south-1 Mumbai).
- **Availability Zone (AZ)**: one or more isolated data centres inside a region.
- A **VPC spans a region**; a **subnet lives in exactly one AZ**.
- Use at least 2 AZs for high availability.
